## 1. What I'm Going to Build

A clean, modern, mobile-friendly website for ROKT with all the pages from your plan:

- **Home** — welcome and overview of ROKT
- **About Us** — your story, mission, vision, and values
- **Programs / Services** — what tutoring you offer and how it works
- **Enroll / Get Help** — a registration form for families
- **Donate / Support Us** — a donation form and giving options
- **Resources for Families** — a place for helpful resources (we'll fill this in together)
- **Contact Us** — a contact form so families can reach you

The site will look professional, work well on phones and computers, and be easy to
update later.

## 2. The Two-Phase Plan

Rather than waiting until everything is perfect to launch, we're going to work in two
stages. This gets ROKT online and accepting support as quickly as possible.

### Phase 1 — Finish the WordPress Site (Right Now)

Your WordPress.com site at roots-of-knowledge.com is already partially built. I'll
finish the remaining pages, forms, and donation options as quickly as possible so you
can start spreading the word, accepting donations, and enrolling children. This phase
prioritizes getting things working over making everything perfect.

### Phase 2 — Upgrade to a Better Platform (Within About 10 Months)

WordPress.com charges an annual subscription fee. Before that subscription renews, I
plan to rebuild the site on a more modern, developer-standard platform. The new site
will look and work identically to visitors — the change is entirely "under the hood."

The benefit to you: **the new platform will cost significantly less to run long-term**
(potentially free for hosting), and the site will be faster, more portable, and easier
to maintain.

---

## 3. How the Site Is Built (in plain terms)

For Phase 2, I'm using a modern, professional approach that web developers call
**"Jamstack."** Here's what that means for you, without the jargon:

- **The site will be fast and secure.** This style of website loads quickly and has fewer
  security weak points than older website systems.
- **Your words live separately from the design.** I keep all the text of your website in
  simple, plain files — separate from the behind-the-scenes code. This separation is the
  key to the next point.
- **You'll be able to edit it yourself later.** When the time is right, I can add a simple
  editing tool that lets you log in, click on a page, change the words, and hit save —
  no coding needed. Because of how I'm building it from the start, adding that tool later
  is straightforward and won't require rebuilding the site.
- **You won't be locked in to any one company.** The site can be moved between hosting
  providers if you ever want to change. You're never trapped.

## 4. Making It Easy for People to Donate

I want supporters to be able to give to ROKT quickly and easily — ideally by clicking a
button and paying by card in a few seconds. I'll set this up using a trusted, secure
service (Stripe) that handles the payment on its own secure page, so your website never
stores anyone's card or bank details.

I'll also keep the Zelle, Cash App, and mailed-check options from your donation sheet,
and I'll make sure your monthly sponsorship options ($30/month for one child, $60/month
for two) work for recurring giving.

**One honest note:** Because ROKT is currently set up as an LLC — not a registered
nonprofit — I am not able to describe donations as "tax-deductible" on the website.
Whether donations to ROKT are tax-deductible for your donors is a legal and tax question;
please check with your accountant or attorney. I'm only handling the website side of
things.

## 6. What This Costs You — and What It Doesn't

**My development work: $0.** I'm not charging for the build described here.

However, **every website has some ongoing running costs.** These are paid by you (or ROKT),
not by me. Here are the relevant costs for each phase:

### Phase 1 — WordPress.com (current, finishing now)

You're already paying for WordPress.com Premium. No new costs for this phase.

| Expense | Estimated cost |
|---|---|
| WordPress.com Premium subscription | Already active — check your renewal date |
| Domain (roots-of-knowledge.com) | Already registered — check renewal date |

### Phase 2 — After Migration (ongoing, much lower cost)

Once the site is moved off WordPress.com, your costs drop significantly:

| Expense | What it's for | Estimated cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Domain name** | Your web address. Paid yearly. | About **$10–$25 per year** |
| **Website hosting** | The service that keeps the site online. | **Free to near-free** on Cloudflare Pages or Netlify for a low-traffic static site |
| **Form delivery** | Sends contact/enrollment form submissions to your email. | **Free** at low volume (Formspree free tier) |
| **Online donations via Stripe** | Lets supporters give by card. | No monthly fee; Stripe keeps ~**2.9% + $0.30 per donation** |
| **Zelle / Cash App / check** | Alternative giving methods. | **Free** |

**Realistic total after migration:** Approximately **$10–$25/year** for the domain,
plus Stripe's per-transaction fee on card donations. Hosting is likely free.
That's a significant saving compared to WordPress.com's annual subscription.
