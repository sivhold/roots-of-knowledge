# ROKT Website Design Brief — Carol's original source document

Carol Bluee's founding brief for the website: the page-by-page plan and the
original wording for mission, vision, values, the founder story, and the
program description. Much of the live site's copy traces back to this document.

**Provenance.** Converted from `docs/website-design.docx` (the folder is now `ai-guidance/source-material/`) on 2026-07-26. That
`.docx` was removed from the repo and its git history because it contained live
WordPress and Pexels passwords in plain text. This markdown version is the
content, with the credentials stripped — it is the only copy that remains.

**Note the drift.** This brief predates two of Carol's decisions, so it does NOT
match the current site and must not be treated as the style guide:

- It says "free high-quality" tutoring with no qualifier. Carol later required
  **"free to qualifying families"** everywhere the site offers tutoring to a
  parent, because children are vetted and selected by ROKT (2026-07-26).
- It says "Donate / Support Us" and "Add donation button". Carol later required
  **sponsorship language site-wide** — ROKT is an LLC and cannot offer
  tax-deductible giving, so "donate"/"donation"/"gift"/"give" are all out
  (2026-07-23, extended 2026-07-26). The route is now `/sponsorship`.
- It mentions "nonprofit partnerships" as a revenue source. ROKT itself is an
  **LLC, not a 501(c)(3)** — never describe ROKT as a nonprofit.

See `CLAUDE.md` for the binding wording rules.

## Page 1 — Home Page

Roots of Knowledge Tutoring LLC (ROKT) is a culturally responsive reading
tutoring service designed to serve students in underserved communities
throughout Los Angeles County. Our mission is to close literacy gaps, strengthen
academic confidence, and empower families by delivering free high-quality
culturally affirming reading instruction. ROKT will provide small-group and
one-on-one tutoring for students in grades K–5, with an initial focus on early
literacy and reading intervention.

Roots of Knowledge Tutoring will operate through a hybrid model, offering
in-person tutoring at community centers, churches, and libraries, as well as
virtual tutoring options. Revenue will be generated through nonprofit
partnerships, grants, and philanthropic support. Our long-term goal is to become
a trusted community-based literacy provider across Los Angeles County.

## Page 2 — About Us

Roots of Knowledge Tutoring (ROKT) was founded by Carol Bluee to address
persistent reading disparities affecting children in low-income and historically
marginalized neighborhoods. After retiring from her executive role at the UCLA
Store, Carol pursued a career in early childhood education. While working in
various schools, she observed that many children did not know their alphabets
and were not prepared to transition to elementary school.

Statistics show that many students in underserved communities read below grade
level, often due to limited access to individualized instruction, culturally
relevant materials, and early intervention services. ROKT was established to
address these gaps by providing instruction grounded in evidence-based literacy
practices and cultural affirmation.

### Mission, Vision, and Values

**Mission:** To improve reading proficiency and academic confidence among
students in underserved areas of Los Angeles County through culturally
responsive, high-quality tutoring.

**Vision:** A future where every child reads confidently, excels academically,
and sees themselves reflected positively in learning.

**Core Values:**

- Equity and access in education
- Cultural pride and representation
- Community partnership and trust
- Academic excellence and accountability

## Page 3 — Programs / Services

At Roots of Knowledge Tutoring, we believe every child deserves access to
high-quality literacy support in an environment where they feel seen, valued,
and encouraged. Tutoring sessions will be available both virtually and in person
at libraries, community centers, and churches, providing flexible options to
meet the needs of students and families. Tutoring will be provided by
trauma-informed individuals who are passionate about supporting students'
reading growth.

What makes our program different is our commitment to combining evidence-based
reading instruction with culturally responsive teaching that reflects the
diverse experiences of the students we serve. We focus on more than academic
growth — we work to build confidence, foster a love of learning, and create
meaningful relationships that help children thrive.

Our small-group model allows each student to receive individualized attention
and targeted support in phonics, reading fluency, and comprehension. We use
structured literacy methods grounded in research while incorporating culturally
relevant books and materials that help students connect personally to reading.
Our trauma-informed, strengths-based approach ensures students learn in a safe
and supportive environment where their abilities are celebrated. In addition, we
partner closely with families by providing parent engagement strategies and
resources that extend learning beyond tutoring sessions and into the home.
